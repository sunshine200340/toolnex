Cookie Policy

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. Here is exactly which ones Toolnex uses and why.

1. Cookies we set directly

CookiePurposeDuration
toolnex_progressStores your local game scores/state in your browser (localStorage). Never sent to our servers.Until cleared
toolnex_consentRemembers your cookie consent choice so we don't ask again.12 months

2. Cookies from advertising partners

If ad personalization is enabled in your region, our advertising partner (Google AdSense) may set cookies such as:

CookiePurposeDuration
__gads / _gacMeasures ad impressions and conversions.Up to 13 months
IDE / NIDSelects relevant ads and limits repeat impressions.Up to 12 months

These are controlled by Google's policies. You can manage them in Google Ads Settings.

3. Analytics cookies

Privacy-focused analytics may set a first-party session cookie to distinguish visitors without identifying you. It carries no advertising data.

4. How to control cookies

  • Use the consent banner (if shown) to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies for toolnex.xyz.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via YourAdChoices.

Disabling some cookies may affect how guides and games remember your progress, but core play still works.
