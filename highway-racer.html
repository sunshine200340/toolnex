Game Overview

Highway Racer puts you behind the wheel on busy multi-lane highways. Dodge traffic, overtake slower vehicles, and race against the clock.

How to Play

  • Use left/right arrows or A/D keys to change lanes.
  • Accelerate automatically — focus on steering and timing.
  • Overtake cars closely for bonus points.
  • Avoid collisions — crashing ends your run immediately.

Quick Tips

  • Stay in the center lane when possible — it offers escape routes on both sides.
  • Anticipate lane changes from AI vehicles.
  • Weave smoothly rather than jerking between lanes.
