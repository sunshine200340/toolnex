Tiny Farm Simulator

Plant, water, harvest - grow a pocket farm from three plots.

Coins: 20Goal: 500
Click a plot to plant, water, grow or harvest

About This Game

Tiny Farm Simulator is a cozy three-by-three field where every plot runs a small lifecycle: plant a seed, water it, watch it sprout, then harvest and sell. Profits buy better seeds, better seeds sell for more, and the loop compounds into a proper little economy.

There is no fail state and no timer - the pleasure is in watching a tidy grid of sprouts turn into a well-oiled harvest machine. The goal marker is 500 coins; reaching it proves you have mastered the rhythm.

How to Play

  1. Click a plot and choose a seed (wheat costs 5 coins, carrots 15).
  2. Click a planted plot to water it - unwatered crops do not grow.
  3. Each click on a growing crop advances it one stage.
  4. Harvest mature crops to sell them; earn 500 coins to win the farm.

Tips & Strategy

  • Wheat is your early engine: cheap in, fast out, steady profit.
  • Reinvest in carrots as soon as you can afford three of them - the margin compounds.
  • Water immediately after planting; idle plots earn nothing.
  • Work in columns: plant the left column, water the middle, harvest the right.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do crops wither if I wait?

Never - Tiny Farm has no withering or real-time waiting. Crops advance when you click them.

Is there an ending?

Reaching 500 coins shows a victory banner, and you can keep playing endlessly after that.

Can I lose the farm?

No fail state exists - the only risk is planting seeds you cannot afford to water.
