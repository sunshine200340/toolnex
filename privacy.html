Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Toolnex collects, what stays on your device, and the choices you have. It applies to toolnex.xyz and all subpages.

1. The short version

Toolnex is built to respect your time and your data. You can play every game and read every guide without creating an account. We do not sell personal information, and we do not require you to hand over an email to play.

2. Data stored on your device (localStorage)

Game progress — such as your high scores, current puzzle state, or farm layout — is saved in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Is tied to the specific browser and device you used.
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data for toolnex.xyz.

Because this data is local, we cannot recover it if you switch devices or clear your browser.

3. Data we collect automatically (analytics)

To understand which guides help players and which games get played most, we use privacy-conscious analytics. This typically records:

  • Pages visited and approximate time on page.
  • Browser type, device category (mobile/desktop), and country (at a regional level).
  • Referring site (e.g., search engine or social link).

We use this in aggregate to improve the site. It is not used to identify you personally.

4. Advertising cookies

Toolnex may display ads through third-party advertising partners (including Google AdSense). These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. See our Cookie Policy for the specific cookies used and how to opt out.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Google Ads Settings or YourAdChoices.

5. Your rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold, and to object to certain processing. If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what data is collected and to request deletion under the CCPA.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our contact page. We respond within the timeframes required by law (generally 30 days).

6. Children's privacy

Toolnex is designed to be family friendly, but it is not directed specifically at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact page. We read every message.
