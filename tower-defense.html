Game Overview

Tower Defense Kingdom is a classic strategy game where players defend their castle from waves of invading enemies by placing defensive towers along the enemy path. Each tower type has unique attack characteristics, ranges, and upgrade paths, requiring thoughtful placement and resource management.

The game features multiple waves of increasing difficulty, with different enemy types including fast runners, heavily armored units, and flying enemies. Players earn gold for each enemy defeated, which can be used to place new towers or upgrade existing ones. The goal is to prevent enemies from reaching the end of the path and breaching the castle gates.

Game Instructions

  • Click on an empty tile next to the path to place a defensive tower
  • Each tower costs gold, which you earn by defeating enemy units
  • Click on an existing tower to upgrade it, increasing its damage, range, and attack speed
  • Arrow towers deal balanced damage at medium range and work well for general defense
  • Cannon towers deal area damage in a splash radius, effective against groups of weak enemies
  • Magic towers can target flying enemies and deal bonus damage to armored units
  • Start each wave manually when you are ready to begin the enemy attack

Strategy & Tips

  • Build at path turns: Towers placed at corners and bends in the path have more time to fire at enemies as they pass.
  • Mix tower types: Do not rely on a single tower type. Combine damage, splash, and anti-air towers to handle all enemy varieties.
  • Upgrade early towers first: Upgrading an existing tower is often more cost-effective than building a new one.
  • Save gold for key waves: Do not spend all your gold immediately. Keep reserves for tough boss waves.
  • Sell misplaced towers: If you place a tower in a poor position, you can sell it for a partial refund to reposition your defenses.
