Castle Guard Defense

Place towers along the invasion path and guard the castle through ten waves.

Gold: 90Wave: 1/10Castle: 10

About This Game

Castle Guard Defense is a friendly introduction to tower defense: a single winding road toward your castle, five buildable plots, two tower types and ten waves of invaders that get meaner as they go.

Archer towers fire fast and cheap; cannon towers hit hard but slowly. Every kill pays a bounty, every tenth enemy in a wave is a fast runner, and the castle holds as long as your economy does. The whole defense fits on one screen - which is the point.

How to Play

  1. Click an empty plot (glowing circles beside the road) to open the build menu.
  2. Buy an Archer Tower (40) for rapid fire or a Cannon Tower (70) for heavy damage.
  3. Click a built tower to see its range; enemies reward gold for every kill.
  4. Survive all 10 waves to win. If 10 enemies reach the castle, the defense falls.

Tips & Strategy

  • Cover the middle stretch of the road where enemies spend the most time.
  • One cannon near a sharp bend outperforms two archers on straights.
  • Save 40 gold during wave one - reacting to wave two's speed matters more.
  • Slow waves are for saving; spend between waves, not during them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell or upgrade towers?

This version keeps one decision clean: where and what to build. Upgrades are planned.

How long is a full game?

Ten waves take roughly six to eight minutes depending on your build.

What are the fast enemies?

Every tenth invader is a runner - twice the speed, so place one tower specifically watching for them.
