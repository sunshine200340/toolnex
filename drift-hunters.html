Game Overview

Drift Hunters lets you master drifting on winding tracks with responsive car physics. Choose vehicles, customize your ride, and earn points by holding long drifts.

How to Play

  • Use arrow keys or WASD to steer, accelerate, and brake.
  • Initiate a drift by turning sharply while applying throttle.
  • Counter-steer to maintain the drift angle through corners.
  • Earn points based on drift duration and angle.

Quick Tips

  • Enter corners at moderate speed — too fast spins out, too slow won't drift.
  • Modulate throttle during the drift: ease off if the angle gets extreme.
  • Practice on wider corners before attempting tight hairpin turns.
