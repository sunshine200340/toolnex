Heavy Duty Warehouse Pallet Racks

Product Introduction

Heavy duty storage pallet racks are high-load industrial warehouse storage equipment designed for high-density goods storage, featuring strong seismic resistance, stable structural performance and long service life. All rack surfaces adopt electrostatic powder spraying treatment, which effectively prevents rust, corrosion, abrasion, adapting to humid, dusty, low-temperature cold storage and other harsh industrial environments, available for both indoor and outdoor warehouse use.

All dimensions and load capacity can be fully customized according to customers' warehouse layout and pallet specifications. The single-layer safe load range is from 1000kg to 3000kg, widely used in manufacturing factories, logistics distribution centers, e-commerce warehouses, cold storage, auto parts warehouses, 3PL third-party logistics enterprises.
