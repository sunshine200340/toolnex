Heavy Duty Pallet Racks for Industrial Warehouse Storage

Product Overview

Heavy duty pallet rack systems are industrial-grade high-load storage equipment specially built for high-density warehouse goods storage. Constructed with thickened cold-rolled steel uprights and box beams, the rack delivers outstanding structural stability and seismic performance, suitable for long-term heavy-load operation in harsh working environments.

All metal surfaces adopt electrostatic powder anti-rust coating, resisting corrosion, dust and moisture, available for indoor warehouses, outdoor storage yards and cold storage facilities. Every dimension, layer count and load capacity can be fully customized to match your warehouse layout and pallet sizes, with single-layer safe bearing capacity ranging from 1000kg to 3000kg. Widely adopted in manufacturing plants, logistics distribution centers, 3PL warehouses, auto parts storage and cold chain facilities.
