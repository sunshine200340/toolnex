Product Overview

The Medium Duty Racking system is engineered for adaptable storage across manufacturing, distribution centers, and retail. Its boltless design and adjustable shelving support rapid assembly and scalable layouts.

Each unit combines robust cold-rolled steel beams and uprights, with a corrosion-resistant finish that keeps your storage system durable in demanding environments. The system is easy to expand by adding more bays or combining main and sub racks.

  • Load range: 300kg to 500kg per shelf
  • Adjustable shelf pitch in 50mm increments
  • Easy boltless assembly with butterfly and diamond hole connectors
  • Wide compatibility with pallet storage, carton organization, and small parts shelving

Why Choose Medium Duty Racking?

Designed for fast-moving goods and versatile storage, this rack is an ideal middle ground between light-duty shelving and heavy-duty pallet racking.

Strong & Stable

The system supports medium loads while maintaining stability with dual-front column support on main racks and single column sub racks.

Quick Assembly

Tool-free assembly and simple part matching make installation fast, reducing onsite labor and downtime.

Flexible Layout

Configure open or closed bays, combine with decking plates, and create varied aisle widths to fit your facility.

Packaging & Shipment

Secure Packing

Clear Labeling

Efficient Shipping

Flat-pack design reduces shipping volume and lowers logistics costs for large orders.
