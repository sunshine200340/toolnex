Iron Plate

Product detail:

The bolt-free rivet shelf is constructed from high-quality cold-rolled steel sheets. It features 5 layers and can support the loading capacity of 150kg per layer. The regular color options are black and white. The production process encompasses punching, bending, forming, spraying, and packaging. The main components include uprights, beams (both long and short), layer boards, and accessories. Layer boards are iron board. The regular thickness of board is 0.4mm, or more thicknesses can be choosed by clinets’ demand. The uprights are 1980mm long with outer hole.

Features:

  1. It can be easily installed by one person. It does not require various accessories such as nuts and screws.
  2. It has a significant advantage in saving shipping space in containers, which greatly reduces product costs.
  3. The surface treatment is electrostatic powder coating. We use high-quality outdoor electrostatic powder plastic, which ensures our shelves are rust-proof and fade-resistant for twenty years.
  4. Our rivetshelves can be widely used in stores, markets, storerooms, garages, studies, offices, showrooms, and warehouses, among other places.
  5. The greatest advantage of our shelves is that they are environmentally friendly, non-toxic, and suitable for pregnant women and infants.

You can adjust the layer distance according to your own needs.

Packaging:

1.10 long beams, 10 short beams and 5 boards will be packed into one cartons. Every 20 columns will be packed separately in PP woven fabric.

2.We can increase bubble pad package outside of carton.
