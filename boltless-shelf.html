Product Overview

The bolt-free rivet shelf is constructed from high-quality cold-rolled steel sheets. It features 5 layers and can support a loading capacity of 150kg per layer. The regular color options are black and white. The production process encompasses punching, bending, forming, spraying, and packaging.[reference:0]

The main components include uprights, beams (both long and short), layer boards, and accessories. Layer boards are iron boards. The regular thickness of the board is 0.4mm, or more thicknesses can be chosen according to client demand. The uprights are 1980mm long with outer holes.[reference:1]

Key Features

  • Easy Installation: Can be easily installed by one person. Does not require various accessories such as nuts and screws.[reference:2]
  • Space-Saving: Significant advantage in saving shipping space in containers, which greatly reduces product costs.[reference:3]
  • Durable Coating: Surface treatment is electrostatic powder coating. High-quality outdoor electrostatic powder plastic ensures shelves are rust-proof and fade-resistant for twenty years.[reference:4]
  • Wide Application: Can be widely used in stores, markets, storerooms, garages, studies, offices, showrooms, and warehouses.[reference:5]
  • Eco-Friendly: Environmentally friendly, non-toxic, and suitable for pregnant women and infants. You can adjust the layer distance according to your own needs.[reference:6]
