About Toolnex

We build original HTML5 browser games and write the guides that help you actually get better at them. No downloads, no accounts, no "open in a new tab" detours.

What Toolnex is

Toolnex started in 2026 as a small side project with a frustration: most "free game" sites are mazes of pop-ups, forced sign-ups, and links that bounce you to three other domains before a game ever loads. We wanted the opposite — a quiet, fast place where you click a game and play it, on the same page, in under a second.

Today Toolnex hosts 18 original games across seven categories — arcade, racing, puzzle, match-3, sports, simulation, and strategy. Every title is written in-house using HTML5 Canvas and plain JavaScript. There is no third-party game iframe on this site, and your progress is saved locally in your browser, not on our servers.

Why we also write guides

A game you can't figure out stops being fun. So alongside the arcade we publish practical, player-tested guides: how to reach 2048 without panic, why your tower placement in Castle Guard Defense matters, the drift line that shaves seconds off Skyline Speed Race. These are written by people who actually played the builds, not spun from a template.

Our editorial rule is simple — if a guide wouldn't help a real player improve in the next five minutes, we don't publish it.

How the games are made

Each game begins as a single mechanic we think is satisfying — a swipe, a merge, a drift — and grows through playtesting on desktop and mobile. We keep the visuals clean and the difficulty curve gentle so the site stays suitable for players of all ages. New titles and guide updates land on a rolling basis; the Games page always shows the current lineup.

Who is behind it

Toolnex is run by a two-person indie team — one developer and one writer — supported by a small group of beta players who break things for us before you ever see them. We're not a large publisher, and we like it that way: it keeps the site focused and the guides honest.

Questions, bug reports, or a guide topic you'd like us to cover? The fastest way to reach us is the contact page.

Our promise to players

  • No accounts, ever. You should never have to register to play a five-minute game.
  • No redirects. What you click is what you play — on this domain.
  • No paywalls or energy timers. Free means free, including the guides.
  • Family friendly. Clean visuals, gentle difficulty, all-ages suitable.
  • Transparent data. Read exactly what we do and don't collect in our Privacy Policy.
