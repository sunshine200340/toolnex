Game Overview

Block Puzzle Classic is a widely acclaimed casual puzzle game designed for players of all age groups. The core gameplay follows the classic tile-stacking mechanic: players place geometric blocks of varying shapes onto a 10x10 grid, and complete full horizontal or vertical lines to clear tiles and accumulate scores.

Distinguished from timed puzzle titles, this game offers a pressure-free casual experience with no time limits. Players can take sufficient time to plan each placement, making it ideal for short work breaks or extended leisure sessions. The game concludes when there is no remaining valid space on the grid to place upcoming blocks.

Game Instructions

  • Drag available blocks from the bottom panel and drop them onto the game grid to place
  • Fill an entire horizontal or vertical row to clear the line and free up grid space
  • Click on blocks to rotate them before placement for optimal positioning
  • Plan placements strategically to avoid running out of available grid space
  • Clear multiple lines simultaneously to earn bonus points and combo multipliers

Strategy & Tips

  • Maintain a flat grid layout: Avoid stacking blocks unevenly in a single area. A level grid provides more flexible placement options for subsequent blocks.
  • Reserve large blocks for combo clears: Keep 3x3 and other large pieces until you can clear multiple lines at once to maximize score efficiency.
  • Reserve gaps for small blocks: Always keep 1-2 single-cell gaps available for randomly appearing 1x1 blocks.
  • Avoid hasty placements: There is no time constraint. Take 2-3 seconds to evaluate all placement options before committing.
  • Prioritize lower line clears: Clearing rows near the bottom first maximizes usable space and extends gameplay duration.
