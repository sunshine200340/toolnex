Contact Us

Found a bug? Want a guide on a specific game? Thinking about a collaboration? Send it our way — we read every message.

Email

The most reliable way to reach us is email: hello@toolnex.xyz. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

Tip: if you're reporting a game that won't load, tell us your browser and device — it speeds up the fix a lot.

Send a message

This opens your email app with the details pre-filled. If it doesn't, just email hello@toolnex.xyz directly.

What to include

  • Bug reports: game name, what you did, what happened, browser + device.
  • Guide requests: which game and what you're stuck on — we prioritize these for the blog.
  • Partnerships: a one-line description of who you are and what you have in mind.
